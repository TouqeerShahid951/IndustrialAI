# Mac CCTV/video test — 2026-10-20

Ran locally on Apple MPS, confidence 0.25, image size 640. Nine useful source clips: one construction-site CCTV video and eight FIRESENSE research clips (mixed fixed-camera, staged and handheld scenes). The additional smoking movie clip is pre-annotated and excluded from accuracy conclusions.

## Findings

- Construction CCTV: helmet boxes appeared in all 90 sampled frames, but NO-Hardhat appeared in only 3. Visual review at 5 seconds shows two nearby helmeted workers detected and a distant bareheaded worker missed. These counts are not worker recall or accuracy.
- The same construction video produced false fire detections in 6/90 sampled frames and no cigarette detections.
- Both fire-positive FIRESENSE clips had detections (91/98 and 79/79 sampled frames). Both smoke-positive clips had detections (44/67 and 70/121 frames). This is detection coverage, not recall; hazards are not necessarily visible throughout every clip.
- One nighttime fire-negative clip produced false fire boxes in 10/52 frames. Three other negative research clips produced no fire/smoke boxes.
- Cigarette detection on genuinely independent, unannotated CCTV remains unvalidated. The downloaded smoking example is a movie montage with existing smoking labels; its 72 boxes do not establish CCTV performance.
- Training overlap is unknown. This small convenience sample is not an independent benchmark. No ground-truth boxes were used, and there are no precision/recall or safety-readiness claims.

## Reproduce

```bash
.venv/bin/python evaluate.py data/cctv/helmet.mp4 --device mps --stride 4 --max-frames 90
.venv/bin/python evaluate.py data/cctv/firesense --models fire_smoke --device mps --stride 3 --max-frames 1000
```

FIRESENSE clips were fetched selectively with scripts/fetch_firesense.py using HTTP ranges and ZIP CRC verification instead of downloading both full archives. Source videos remain in data/cctv/.
