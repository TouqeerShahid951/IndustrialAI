# Dataset inventory and remaining work

Updated 23 October 2026. Read with [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md). This records what was actually acquired, inspected, annotated, fitted or evaluated during this project, and what is left. Counts are from the linked local reports; access findings describe those dated checks, not a promise of current availability.

**No candidate has qualified to replace the application's default models. No deployment-site footage is available; continue with public sources.** Downloaded data is not automatically training data, and related clips/frames do not constitute independent cameras or incidents. The same material appears in several experiments; do not sum the rows into a unique dataset total.

## What is left, in order

1. **Acquire new public camera families:** verified steam/mist/dust sources, normal activity, clear backlit scenes, faint/developing smoke and genuine fire, with known source context and original timing. New factories/sites matter more than more neighboring frames from the same laboratory.
2. **Freeze camera/site/incident roles before sampling:** fitting, validation and a genuinely unseen test. Audit cross-dataset copies and keep source-family versions together. Never treat the remaining MEVA or MmodalFire frames as independent test data.
3. **Complete useful pending annotations:** B03–B06, remaining W clips, and other selected source intervals. Uncertain L01/L02 process haze must remain uncertain without supporting source evidence. These additions improve the development set, not blind-test independence. Larger archives such as FASDD remain only partly visually audited.
4. **Improve weak smoke without hiding false alarms:** B02 remains missed by temporal verification; clip-007's title card and clear street produce false flags. Build corresponding *new* development examples, calibrate only on validation and repeat fixed controls. More training epochs alone is not the established solution.
5. **Obtain enough separately reviewed negative exposure and independent positive events:** current 230.15 s of labeled negative time is too short for an operational false-alarm rate; one workshop supplies most smoke-positive samples. Report per-source/event performance and uncertainty.
6. **Address the other capabilities with their own data:** positive smoking CCTV and confusing actions; distant/occluded heads and explicit no-helmet negatives; independently labeled zone crossings/occlusions. All-five integration and factory behavior labels do not establish accuracy for these tasks.
7. **Independent label review and provenance checks before qualification:** current new labels are largely single-assistant reviews, chemical aerosol composition is often unknown and public pretraining overlap is unresolved. Only then consider a default-model change and full MVP regression checks.
